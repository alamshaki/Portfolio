Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What technologies do you use for web development?
I work with WordPress, PHP, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and various frameworks and libraries. Depending on your project needs, I can also integrate APIs, databases, and third-party tools.
What services do you offer as a web developer?
I specialize in developing custom websites, WordPress themes, and plugins using PHP, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Whether you need a new website, improvements to an existing one, or custom functionality, I can help.
Will my website be mobile-friendly?
Definitely! I ensure all websites are fully responsive, meaning they will work seamlessly on desktops, tablets, and smartphones.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes! I can revamp your website with a modern design, improved functionality, and optimized performance while ensuring it meets the latest web standards.
How much does a website cost?
The cost varies based on the project's scope, features, and complexity. A basic website starts at [$50], while custom development and advanced features may increase the price. Contact me for a tailored quote.
How long does a website project usually take?
Typical website projects take 2-6 weeks depending on the complexity, number of pages, and client feedback rounds. Simple landing pages can be completed faster, while custom systems and e-commerce sites may require more time.
Do you provide ongoing support and maintenance?
Yes. I offer ongoing support, maintenance, updates, and performance optimization packages to keep your website secure, fast, and up to date after launch.
